# The Role of AI in Modern Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is a constant cat-and-mouse game. As attackers become more sophisticated, defenders must leverage every tool at their disposal. **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Machine Learning (ML)** have become indispensable in this fight.

## My Experience

During my time as a Team Lead for a stealth startup, I managed a team of developers focused on creating AI-driven cybersecurity tools. We achieved best-in-class accuracy for detecting anomalies in network traffic.

## Why AI?

AI excels at:
* Identifying patterns in massive datasets
* Detecting novel threats that don't match known signatures
* Reducing false positives through continuous learning

## The Future

I believe that the next generation of security tools will be fully autonomous, capable of identifying and neutralizing threats in real-time without human intervention.
