LBEF Business Intelligence System Slides

This page will have the slides from each session and link to other folders

📊

Session 1 - Introduction to Business Intelligence System

The session covered the basics of business intelligence system with concepts such as science, computing, IT core capabilities, big data and its characterstics, history of business intelligence and capabilities of Business Intelligence System.

📊

Session 2 - BI Tools

The slides covers the pros and cons of different BI solutions, evaluate the available BI alternatives in the market, understand modern BI trends and emerging technologies and assessing information challenges and best practices.

📊

Session 3 - Organization Memory

The slides covers the concepts of Organization Memory, need of BI, BIS Capabilities, Types of Data, Stages of Organization Memory, AI Powered-Organization Memory, Metadata and Data Lineage Management, Data Governance Framework, Knowledge Management Integration, Cloud-Based Systems, Challenges and Best Practices, and Future Trends of BIS.

📊

Session 4 - Text and Web Mining

The slides covers the concepts of Organization Memory, need of BI, BIS Capabilities, Types of Data, Stages of Organization Memory, AI Powered-Organization Memory, Metadata and Data Lineage Management, Data Governance Framework, Knowledge Management Integration, Cloud-Based Systems, Challenges and Best Practices, and Future Trends of BIS.

📄

Session 5 - AGGORA Case Discussion

The idea of this case study is to understand how Business Intelligence Systems are adopted in companies and how it can be better promoted.

📊

Session 6 - Data Integration and ETL

The slides covers the concepts of Data Integration and how they are performed, ETL and ELT Process and two videos to illustrate these ideas

📊

Session 7 - Insight Creation

The slides covers the concepts of insight creation and how data are converted into valuable insights for businesses to use for their growth. It talks about the process of insight creation and what tools are used to perform it.
